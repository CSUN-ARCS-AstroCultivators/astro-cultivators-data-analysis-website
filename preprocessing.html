Data Cleaning and Preprocessing

This stage prepares raw hyperspectral, RGB, and depth images for segmentation, feature extraction, modeling, and visualization.

Preprocessing Goal

The goal was to turn raw image folders into clean and structured data. This included validating hyperspectral cubes, filtering unusable RGB and depth frames, keeping lights on captures, removing corrupted images, and preparing plant masks.

Hyperspectral Preprocessing

The hyperspectral preprocessing work cleaned raw TIFF files and created image metadata for later feature engineering.

Filename Parsing

Messy filenames were parsed to extract dates and sequence numbers.

Band Validation

Each image was checked for 51 spectral bands across the expected wavelength range.

NDVI Computation

NDVI was computed from red and near infrared bands to help isolate plant pixels.

Vegetation Masking

NDVI thresholding and morphological cleanup were used to create clean plant masks.

Key outputs included cleaned dataset indexes, verified 51 band images, mask ratio, mean NDVI, median NDVI, and other metadata CSV files.

HS Feature Pipeline Filtering

For the January Set 1 feature workflow, hyperspectral frames were filtered to make sure each record was useful for feature computation.

  • Images were filtered to the 6 AM to 1 PM lights on window.
  • Quality checks included signal to noise ratio, coverage, and mean NDVI.
  • Plant pixels were segmented with NDVI thresholding.
  • The final HS feature output included 204 timestamped records across 21 days.

RealSense RGB and Depth Preprocessing

The RealSense preprocessing work used Intel RealSense D435 RGB and 16 bit raw depth data. The pipeline cleaned frames before segmentation and feature extraction.

Lights On Filtering

Frames were filtered to the 6 AM to 1 PM lights on window for more consistent imaging.

Brightness Check

Dim or corrupted frames were rejected when mean RGB brightness was below 50.

Depth Selection

The pipeline used the 16 bit raw depth file and discarded the 8 bit depth visualization.

Quality Filtering

Quality filtering helped remove blur, bad detections, and unusable frames before modeling.

Grounded SAM 2 Plant Segmentation

Grounded SAM 2 was used for zero shot segmentation with the text prompt soybean plant. This allowed plant level segmentation without training a custom segmentation model.

  • Grounded SAM 2 detected soybean plants from the RGB image.
  • SAM 2.1 generated the plant mask from each detected box.
  • Control plants were assigned to the left side and drought plants to the right side by detection box center.
  • HSV color filtering was used as a backup refinement to remove pots and clean leaf edges.
  • The final pipeline kept 265 usable per plant frames after filtering.

Preprocessing Outputs

  • Cleaned hyperspectral image indexes and metadata files.
  • Verified HS images with valid 51 band structure.
  • RealSense RGB and raw depth frames filtered by light and image quality.
  • Plant masks from Grounded SAM 2 and SAM 2.1.
  • Separated control and drought plant regions for feature extraction.
